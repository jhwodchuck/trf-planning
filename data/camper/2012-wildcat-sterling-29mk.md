# 2012 Forest River Wildcat Sterling Edition 29MK

Status: **working identification—not yet field verified**

## Model-name conflict

The camper has been referred to as a `Wildcat Sterling Edition 293MK`. Published 2012 model data distinguishes:

- **Wildcat Sterling Edition 29MK**, and
- **Wildcat 293MK**, a different non-Sterling listing.

Do not merge dimensions from the two models. Confirm the exact designation from the camper's VIN/data plate, title, or original paperwork before using this baseline for approval.

## Base footprint calculation

Using 31 ft 7 in × 8 ft:

- Length: `31 + 7/12 = 31.583 ft`
- Body rectangle: `31.583 × 8 = 252.7 sq ft`

This **252.7 sq ft** value is not the deployed campsite footprint. It excludes slide-outs, stairs, awning, hitch maneuvering space, stabilizers, utilities, generator separation, and walking/emergency routes.

The published floor plan appears to show:

- hitch/nose at the right side of the source image;
- entry door on the lower side of the source image;
- a large living/dining slide on the upper side;
- a bedroom slide on the upper side;
- a rear recliner slide at the left/rear.

For the requested site orientation, rotate this camper geometry **90 degrees clockwise**:

- hitch/nose points **south**;
- entry door faces **west**;
- living/dining and bedroom slides project **east**, toward the assumed road side;
- rear recliner slide projects **north**.

This orientation makes the road-side slide projections and 10-ft roadway setback especially important.

## Openings and hazards to map

Record the location and height of each:

- entry door;
- emergency exit;
- every operable window;
- roof vents and A/C equipment;
- furnace exhaust/intake;
- water-heater exhaust/intake;
- refrigerator vents;
- propane compartment;
- battery compartment;
- shore-power inlet;
- freshwater connection/fill;
- sewer outlets;
- exterior cooking equipment, if present.

These locations are mandatory inputs to the generator and emergency-egress layout.
