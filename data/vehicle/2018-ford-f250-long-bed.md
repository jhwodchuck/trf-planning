# 2018 Ford F-250 Long-Bed Tow Vehicle

Status: **cab configuration and actual dimensions not yet verified**

The vehicle is a 2018 Ford F-250 with an 8-ft/long bed. Overall length varies materially by cab, so `F-250 long bed` is not sufficient for a scaled site plan.

## Published length candidates

Published 2018 Super Duty dimensions indicate approximately:

| Configuration with 8-ft box | Overall length | Feet |
|---|---:|---:|
| Regular Cab | 231.8 in | 19.32 ft |
| SuperCab | 254.4 in | 21.20 ft |
| Crew Cab | 266.2 in | 22.18 ft |

For a Crew Cab long-bed configuration, published wheelbase is approximately 176 in.

These dimensions are planning references only. Bumpers, steps, hitch equipment, tires, mirrors, or aftermarket equipment can alter the operating envelope.

Sources are maintained in [`../../docs/source-index.md`](../../docs/source-index.md).

## Site-planning questions

- Must the truck remain inside the assigned plot after setup?
- Is separate participant parking available or required?
- Can the truck temporarily enter the roadway setback during setup without blocking traffic?
- Can the camper be backed into the site in the final orientation without crossing neighboring reserved areas?
- Where will the truck sit while the camper slides and canopy are deployed?
- Can the truck reconnect and pull the camper out if neighboring plots are already occupied?

## Maneuvering envelope

The final plan needs more than a parked-truck rectangle. It should include a separate setup/move-out drawing showing:

- approach direction;
- trailer wheel path;
- truck front swing;
- trailer tail swing;
- clearance to trees and boundaries;
- hitching/unhitching position;
- landing-gear deployment;
- temporary truck position after unhitching;
- exit path at the end of the season.

A practical onsite test using cones or tape at the camper's measured dimensions is preferable to relying on a generic turning-radius calculation.
