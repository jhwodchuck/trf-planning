## Planning intent

Treat the two passes as two independent **600 sq ft** allocations. They may form one 1,200 sq ft envelope or separate, stepped, or irregular polygons. Do not force a compact shape when disconnected polygons preserve more useful claimed ground around the camper, shower trailer, road, trees, drainage, or neighboring sites.

The preferred concepts to test are:

1. **Camper polygon + shower polygon:** place the camper and most deployed features in one 600 sq ft polygon and the shower trailer in the second 600 sq ft polygon.
2. **Shared irregular envelope:** place both structures inside one irregular 1,200 sq ft combined allocation.
3. **Group-access shower placement:** use a disconnected shower-trailer polygon near shared pedestrian access while preserving privacy, drainage control, service access, and emergency circulation.

Gaps between allocation polygons are acceptable and should remain outside the calculated area. Compactness is not a planning goal.

The published camper length of 36 ft 9 in includes the coupler and should not be treated as a simple rectangular body footprint. Likewise, the shower trailer must not be represented as a 16 ft rectangle with an assumed width. Final geometry requires field measurements for both units and all deployed systems.

## Drawing rule

Test both contiguous and disconnected 600 sq ft pass allocations. Label every polygon component and its area.

Use separate provisional symbols for:

- `SHANE & SABRINA CAMPER — DEPLOYED DIMENSIONS REQUIRED`
- `16 FT FLATBED SHOWER TRAILER — WIDTH / OVERALL LENGTH / UTILITIES REQUIRED`

Prefer the arrangement that maximizes usable claimed area while providing safe shower access, privacy, drainage control, utility servicing, and camper operation rather than compactness or symmetry.
