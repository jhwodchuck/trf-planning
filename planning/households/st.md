## Planning intent

The tent, rainfly, guy lines, handicap portapotty, entrances, paths, and support equipment must fit within the 600 sq ft household allocation. The arrangement follows the same general pattern as the shower-pass fire-pit concept: a larger equipment end, a long narrow connector, and a second equipment end. The nominal equipment footprints leave 364 sq ft for buffers, the connector path, entry/turning space, seating, storage, and other circulation.

The 4 ft connector is a planning allowance, not a confirmed accessible route. Its finished clear width, surface, slope, turns, obstructions, and connection to the portapotty door must be verified before treating it as accessible.
