## Planning intent

Treat the two passes as two independent **600 sq ft** allocations that may be contiguous, separated, stepped, or irregular. Do not force both tents into one compact boundary when separate polygons preserve more usable claimed area.

Both tents, rainflies, guy lines, entrances, paths, and support equipment must remain inside the combined 1,200 sq ft of requested polygons. Unallocated gaps between those polygons are acceptable and should not cause either pass allocation to be reduced.
