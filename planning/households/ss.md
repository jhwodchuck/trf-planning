## Planning intent

Treat the two passes as two independent **600 sq ft** allocations. They may form one 1,200 sq ft envelope or separate, stepped, or irregular polygons. Do not force a compact shape when disconnected polygons preserve more useful claimed ground around the camper, shower trailer, road, trees, drainage, or neighboring sites.

### Selected working camper-pass concept

The camper pass is an irregular 600 sq ft polygon designed around the floorplan-informed operating sides:

- a **12 × 40 ft base strip** contains the camper body and both roadside slide estimates: **480 sq ft**;
- a curbside **3 × 8 ft rear service/step bay** supports the rear entry and outside-kitchen area: **24 sq ft**; and
- a curbside **8 × 12 ft main-entry rug bay** provides a provisional outdoor living zone: **96 sq ft**.

Total: **600 sq ft**. The camper is oriented with both slides on one side and both doors, steps, awning, outside kitchen, and rug bays on the opposite side. The rug is currently a working **8 × 12 ft assumption** and must be replaced with its actual dimensions.

The current drawing shows both doors and provisional steps. It does not prove that the awning, appliance doors, griddle, stabilizers, utilities, or actual step projections fit; those measurements remain required.

The preferred concepts to test are:

1. **Camper polygon + shower polygon:** place the camper and most deployed features in one 600 sq ft polygon and the shower trailer in the second 600 sq ft polygon.
2. **Shared irregular envelope:** place both structures inside one irregular 1,200 sq ft combined allocation.
3. **Group-access shower placement:** use a disconnected shower-trailer polygon near shared pedestrian access while preserving privacy, drainage control, service access, and emergency circulation.

Gaps between allocation polygons are acceptable and should remain outside the calculated area. Compactness is not a planning goal.

The published camper length of 36 ft 9 in includes the coupler and should not be treated as a simple rectangular body footprint. Pass B is now an exact 600 sq ft reattached concept: a notched 393 sq ft shower/water section, a routed 105 sq ft connector, and a 102 sq ft fire-pit end. The sideways shower trailer and perpendicular IBC water trailer remain inside the shower section. Final geometry and all fire/operating clearances still require field measurements and approval.
