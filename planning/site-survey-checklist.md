# Field Site Survey Checklist

Do not dig, drive hardware, or attach anything to trees merely to survey the site.

## Identification

- [ ] Survey date/time and surveyors recorded.
- [ ] Official map/source revision recorded.
- [ ] Havok-area requested location marked.
- [ ] Plot ID or approval reference recorded.
- [ ] GPS coordinate near plot center recorded.
- [ ] Weather and ground condition recorded.

## Boundary and road

- [ ] Assign corner IDs `P1`, `P2`, etc.
- [ ] Measure every side and both diagonals where applicable.
- [ ] Record bearings/directions.
- [ ] Identify the physical road edge.
- [ ] Measure and photograph the 10-ft roadway setback.
- [ ] Map entrances, intersections, fire lanes, and neighboring boundaries.

## Elevation and drainage

- [ ] Establish a documented relative-elevation datum.
- [ ] Record elevation at corners and intended wheel/landing-gear points.
- [ ] Mark high/low points, ditches, swales, ruts, ponding, erosion, and flow paths.
- [ ] Note ground firmness and visible water/debris marks.
- [ ] Photograph terrain from low angles in both principal directions.

## Obstacles and vegetation

For each tree, root mass, stump, branch, pole, ditch, rock, or fixed obstacle:

- [ ] Assign an ID.
- [ ] Locate it from two measured references.
- [ ] Record footprint/diameter.
- [ ] Record lowest overhead clearance.
- [ ] Photograph with a scale reference.

## Camper mock placement

- [ ] Mark measured body corners, hitch, and rear extremity.
- [ ] Mark all three slides.
- [ ] Mark stairs/landing, awning, stabilizers, and utilities.
- [ ] Check the nearest deployed object against the road setback.
- [ ] Verify emergency-exit clearance and leveling feasibility.

## Truck access

- [ ] Record arrival direction.
- [ ] Test/mark trailer wheel path, truck front swing, and trailer tail swing.
- [ ] Verify overhead clearance.
- [ ] Mark unhitching and temporary truck positions.
- [ ] Prove an exit path with neighboring sites occupied.

## Generator feasibility

Do not operate generators for the survey unless all safety requirements are already satisfied.

- [ ] Mark every camper door, operable window, vent, and exhaust.
- [ ] Mark neighboring occupied openings/areas.
- [ ] Test 20-ft separation from openings for feasibility.
- [ ] Check manufacturer combustible and airflow clearances.
- [ ] Identify unobstructed exhaust direction.
- [ ] Mark separate fuel storage and extinguisher.
- [ ] Test screen placement without creating an enclosure.
- [ ] Route cords away from roadways and emergency paths.

## Canopy and screening

- [ ] Mark canopy roof, legs, feet, guy lines, stakes, or ballast.
- [ ] Verify door/stair and roof-runoff clearance.
- [ ] Mark fence panels, feet/posts, and gate swing.
- [ ] Preserve an emergency route and generator airflow.
- [ ] Confirm no tree attachment or unapproved digging.

## Photographs

- [ ] Location marked on official map.
- [ ] Plot from road and road from plot.
- [ ] Each corner toward center.
- [ ] Every boundary/setback with tape visible.
- [ ] Every obstacle, high/low point, and drainage path.
- [ ] Camper approach and exit.
- [ ] Proposed generator direction and neighboring relationship.

## Closeout

- [ ] Area calculated from measured geometry.
- [ ] Measurements checked for transcription errors.
- [ ] Photos backed up and consistently named.
- [ ] Coordinates copied in decimal degrees.
- [ ] Assumptions listed separately.
- [ ] No markers or trash left without authorization.
- [ ] Data entered into the repository.
