# TRF Planning

Planning documents and site-plan assets for Texas Renaissance Festival camping.

## Planning records

- [TRF site-planning map pack](maps/README.md) — official campground reference, interactive aerial/topographic/GIS viewer, source catalog, GeoJSON area, and automated static-map builder
- [Interactive map viewer](maps/viewer/index.html) — aerial imagery, USGS topography, parcels, streets, FEMA flood zones, wetlands, drawing, measurement, and GeoJSON import/export
- [Dimensioned campsite footprint](maps/overlays/requested-campsite-footprint.svg) — scale-controlled 31 ft 6 in RV zone and 10 x 15 ft canopy template
- [Cummins Onan P4500i generator inventory](equipment/cummins-onan-p4500i.md) — specifications and readiness checklist for the two generators on hand
- [Anker SOLIX S2000 power station inventory](equipment/anker-solix-s2000.md) — specifications, charging limits and camper-use planning for the battery power station on hand
