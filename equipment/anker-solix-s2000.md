## Battery life

Anker publishes the following cycle-life ratings:

- **6,000 cycles to 80%** of original capacity
- **10,000 cycles to 60%** of original capacity

Actual capacity and lifespan depend on load, temperature, charging habits and storage conditions.

## TRF and camper planning notes

- Treat **1,500 W as the continuous AC limit**. The 3,000 W figure is a short peak rating, not sustained output.
- This unit is not large enough to replace either P4500i for normal full-camper operation.
- It may power an RV air conditioner only if the air conditioner's measured running draw remains below 1,500 W and startup is accepted by the inverter. Hot-weather compressor draw can leave little or no usable headroom.
- A nominal 2,010 Wh battery does not deliver the full nameplate capacity as 120 V AC energy because of inverter losses, internal consumption, temperature and reserve limits.
- For rough planning, usable AC energy may be estimated at approximately **1.6–1.8 kWh**, but actual testing with the intended load should control.
- Approximate runtime before compressor cycling and other variables:
  - 100 W average load: about 16–18 hours
  - 500 W average load: about 3.2–3.6 hours
  - 1,000 W average load: about 1.6–1.8 hours
  - 1,500 W average load: about 1.0–1.2 hours
- It is well suited to overnight electronics, lights, fans, CPAP equipment, internet equipment and a refrigerator when their combined draw stays under the inverter limit.
- The S2000 can be recharged from a P4500i. Standard 1,150 W charging is easier on generator capacity than 1,600 W UltraFast charging and leaves more generator headroom for other loads.
- Keep the battery dry, shaded and out of direct Texas sun. Do not store or operate it where rain, standing water or excessive heat can reach it.
