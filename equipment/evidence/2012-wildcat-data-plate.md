## Identification conclusion

The manufacturer model code ends in **`29MK`**, resolving the repository's `29MK` versus `293MK` conflict in favor of **29MK**. The camper should no longer be labeled `293MK` in plans or equipment records.

The label does not state the consumer-facing **Sterling Edition** name and does not provide overall body or slide-out dimensions. Existing published Sterling 29MK references remain useful and model-aligned, but all exterior, deployed, stair, awning, and stabilizer dimensions still require direct measurement before an approval drawing is treated as final.
