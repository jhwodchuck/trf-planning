## Site-planning role

The trailer should be shown as a separate major structure from the camper. Because S. and S. have two passes, test layouts in which:

1. the camper occupies one 600 sq ft pass polygon and the shower trailer occupies the second 600 sq ft polygon;
2. both structures share one irregular 1,200 sq ft envelope; and
3. the shower trailer sits in a disconnected polygon closer to shared group access while preserving privacy, drainage control, and emergency circulation.

Compactness is not a goal. Prefer the arrangement that preserves more usable claimed area and gives the shower facility safe pedestrian access, privacy, water handling, and maintenance access.

## Shower-facility planning requirements

The final layout should show:

- the trailer body, tongue, wheels, jack, stabilizers, and leveling pads;
- the shower enclosure and roof or canopy footprint;
- dry entry/exit footing and a non-slip walking path;
- privacy screening and door/curtain opening direction;
- fresh-water supply, fill access, pump, heater, propane, and electrical equipment;
- greywater capture and disposal routing;
- tank access for filling, draining, removal, or pumping;
- drainage direction and protection against runoff entering neighboring camps or roads;
- fire and ventilation clearance around any water heater or propane equipment;
- nighttime lighting without creating a trip hazard;
- emergency access and a clear route back to the shared camp; and
- all hoses, cords, stakes, guy lines, ballast, and service clearances.
