# 2012 Forest River Wildcat Dimensional Baseline

Status: **working identification; field verification required**

## Conflicts to resolve

The camper has been described as a `Wildcat Sterling Edition 293MK`, while published 2012 data distinguishes:

- **Wildcat Sterling Edition 29MK**, and
- **Wildcat 293MK**, a different non-Sterling model.

The current map template also uses a **31 ft 6 in × 8 ft 4 in** body, while published Sterling 29MK sources commonly report about **31 ft 7 in × 8 ft**. Do not silently choose between these values. Confirm the VIN/data plate and measure the actual camper.

## Floor plan and requested rotation

![2012 Wildcat Sterling 29MK floor plan](https://spec.dlrwebservice.com/3/rv/fp/2012-Forest-River-Wildcat-Sterling-29MK-fp.png)

The source floor plan shows the nose at right, entry on the lower side, two side slides on the upper side, and a rear slide at left. Rotating the camper geometry **90 degrees clockwise** produces the requested arrangement:

- nose/hitch south;
- entry door and stairs west, away from the adjacent road;
- living/dining and bedroom slides east, toward the road;
- rear slide north.

The east-side slides—not only the camper wall—must remain clear of the roadway setback.
