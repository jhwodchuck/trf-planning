# S. and S. — KZ Connect SE C312BHKSE Floor Plan

Status: **working identification; model year and VIN/data plate still require verification**

The supplied camper photographs match the **KZ Connect SE C312BHKSE** layout closely. The 2021 manufacturer floor plan is being used as the current planning reference because its exterior graphics, two-door arrangement, two slide-outs, front queen bedroom, rear bunkhouse, and outside kitchen agree with the photographed camper.

The published exterior length runs from the rear bumper to the leading edge of the coupler. It should not be treated as a rectangular body length when calculating occupied ground area.

## Layout relevant to site planning

With the hitch/nose shown at the right of the manufacturer drawing:

- the queen bedroom is at the front;
- the rear bunkhouse is at the left;
- the 80-inch U-dinette and 64-inch sofa form the large roadside slide;
- the bunkhouse dinette forms the second slide;
- the awning, main steps, second exterior door, outside kitchen, and outside griddle are on the curb/door side;
- pass-through storage is at the front.

## Current site-pass concept

The current 600 sq ft camper-pass SVG orients the estimated slides on the roadside and the two floorplan-derived doors on the curbside. It adds:

- a provisional 3 × 8 ft rear service/step bay for the rear entry, outside kitchen, and griddle;
- a provisional 8 × 12 ft rug bay beside the main entry;
- both unmeasured step projections; and
- the published 18 ft awning length with its deployed projection still marked unknown.

The 8 × 12 ft rug is a planning assumption, not a confirmed household measurement. See [`../maps/overlays/passes/ss_camper.svg`](../maps/overlays/passes/ss_camper.svg).
