# Texas Renaissance Festival Map and Terrain Planning Guidance

Last reviewed: **2026-07-27**

## Official campground map

- [Fields of New Market Campground Map](https://www.texrenfest.com/p/camping-and-more/camping/campground-map)
- [2024 official downloadable map image currently linked by TRF](https://cdn.saffire.com/files.ashx?t=fg&rid=TexasRenFest&f=2024_TRF_Campground_Map_LARGE.jpg)

The campground map is maintained by the Texas Renaissance Festival. Check the official page for revisions before every submission.

## Appropriate use

The official map is suitable for:

- selecting and communicating a general requested location;
- identifying roads, entrances, campground regions, and landmarks;
- attaching a marked location to the TRF request form;
- orienting field-survey work.

It is **not** a survey-grade parcel drawing and should not be used to infer exact plot dimensions, road setbacks, tree positions, slope, drainage, or generator clearances.

## Terrain and environmental screening

After a precise plot location or coordinates are available, check:

- [USGS National Map Viewer](https://www.usgs.gov/tools/national-map-viewer) for imagery, elevation data, and contours;
- [USGS 3DEP products](https://www.usgs.gov/3d-elevation-program/about-3dep-products-services) for elevation-model provenance and available resolution;
- [USDA NRCS Web Soil Survey](https://www.nrcs.usda.gov/resources/data-and-reports/web-soil-survey) for soil type and drainage/engineering limitations;
- [FEMA Flood Map Service Center](https://msc.fema.gov/portal/home) for effective mapped flood-hazard screening.

These sources support screening and planning but do not replace an onsite survey. Small ditches, ruts, roots, local ponding, and temporary road-edge conditions may not appear in regional datasets.

## Required plot-specific map set

Create these files after the exact requested or assigned location is known:

1. `location-on-official-map` — official map with the requested area clearly marked.
2. `field-base-map` — measured boundaries, road edge, setback, north arrow, obstacles, and elevation points.
3. `terrain-screening-map` — available elevation/contour, soil, drainage, and flood references with source dates.
4. `setup-access-plan` — truck/trailer approach, swing, unhitching, and exit.
5. `deployed-site-plan` — camper slides, stairs, canopy, fence, generators, paths, and utilities.
6. `generator-clearance-plan` — openings, exhaust direction, safety zones, fuel, and screen.

## Drawing metadata

Every exported map or drawing should include:

- title;
- revision and date;
- source list and source dates;
- north arrow;
- numerical scale and scale bar;
- paper size;
- plot area calculation;
- coordinate reference or field datum;
- legend distinguishing verified, published, scale-derived, and assumed geometry.
