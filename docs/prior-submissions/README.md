# 2025 TRF Submission Reference

These files are public-safe reference copies of the package J. Harmon emailed to the Texas Renaissance Festival camping office on **September 25, 2025**. Because this repository is public, phone numbers, personal email addresses, and handwritten signatures have been redacted. The location, rules, dimensions, camper information, and other planning content were retained.

## Files

- [2025 land-request submission](2025-trf-land-request-submission-public-redacted.pdf) - completed land-request form, acknowledged site rules, requested-location map near Havok, and the proposed campsite layout.
- [2025 signed season-pass standards](2025-trf-season-pass-standards-signed-public-redacted.pdf) - the acknowledged 2025 season-pass standards, with the handwritten signature redacted.

## What the 2025 package records

- The 2025 form stated a maximum of **525 sq ft per season-pass holder**.
- The proposed fenced rectangle was **32 ft 2 in x 16 ft 3 in**, or about **522.7 sq ft**.
- The camper was listed as one **31.5 ft Forest River RV Wildcat Sterling Edition 293MK**.
- The requested-location map placed the proposed site in relation to **Havok** and the adjacent access road.
- The package was signed and submitted on **September 25, 2025**.

## How to use this for 2026

Use these records for continuity and comparison, not as current authority. The current 2026 forms, rules, and written TRF approval control the new request.

The 2025 drawing did not separately show the truck, canopy, generators, fuel storage, emergency route, or all deployed clearances. Those items should be included or explicitly resolved in the 2026 package rather than copying the old submission unchanged.

The original unredacted attachments remain in the sent email and are intentionally not stored in this public repository.
