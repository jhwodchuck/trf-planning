# Informal Messenger drafts for clan leaders

Last updated: **2026-09-05**

Status: **Drafts only — no messages sent.**

Use these in existing one-to-one conversations with leaders Jason actually knows. Replace `[Name]` and `[Clan]` privately. No recipient identities or relationships were verified during this review, so none are invented here. A clan appearing on the public map is not evidence that Jason knows its leader.

## 1. General first message to a leader you know

Hey [Name] — I've been building a TRF clan map so “come find us” doesn't turn into a three-hour side quest: https://trf-map.vercel.app/

Would you take a look at [Clan] and tell me what I've got wrong? The starting pins are from last year's map, not confirmed spots for this year. You can use the form, but honestly, just send me a pin or scribble on a screenshot if that's easier. Anything off with the name, location, or the site itself, tell me directly. I'd rather get it right with you than guess.

## 2. Very short version for a close friend

Hey [Name], I made a thing: https://trf-map.vercel.app/

Can you sanity-check [Clan] for me? Those are old starting pins, not me declaring where you're camping this year. Wrong name, wrong spot, broken button — just message me. A screenshot with a circle on it absolutely counts as cartography.

## 3. For a clan whose existing listing you have checked

Hey [Name] — [Clan] is on the starting version of the map: https://trf-map.vercel.app/

It's an approximate pin from the 2025 map. Is the name right, and is that anywhere near your plans for 2026? Send me a better pin or screenshot if it needs moving. Also let me know what you're comfortable having shown publicly. This is just my community project, not anything official from TRF.

Editorial condition: use this only after checking that the clan is actually listed. An exact claim link can be copied from the live clan list; never invent its slug.

## 4. For a missing clan

Hey [Name] — I'm putting together a community clan map, and I don't want to leave [Clan] out: https://trf-map.vercel.app/

Are you happy to have the clan listed? If so, send me the name you use and a pin or screenshot of the area you're planning for 2026. Nothing firm yet is a perfectly good answer. And if you'd rather stay off the public map, tell me — no pressure.

## 5. Reply when someone reports a bad pin or broken site

Thanks for catching that. Send me a screenshot and what it should show, and I'll check it against the map. If it's the website acting up, let me know whether you opened it inside Facebook or in your normal browser. No need to put personal details in a public comment.

Editorial condition: after investigation, send a factual update. Do not say “fixed” or “approved” until the change is actually live and checked.

## 6. Reply when their plans are still uncertain

No problem — I won't treat “probably there” as a confirmed location. Send me the details when you know more, or tell me if you want the old marker removed in the meantime. This is supposed to help people find you, not lock you into a spot.

## 7. Reply to a removal/privacy request

Understood. Which clan name or marker should I check? A screenshot is fine; you don't need to fill out a new location form just to ask for removal. I'll review it and get back to you here. If a saved edition has already been shared, I can update future editions, but I can't recall copies people have already downloaded.

## 8. One gentle follow-up, when appropriate

Hey [Name] — circling back once on the clan map. No rush if camp plans are still up in the air. Mostly checking that I haven't put [Clan] in the wrong place or mangled the name. You can just reply here with a correction; no form required.

## Private personalization and handling

Before sending, confirm the recipient's preferred name, correct clan, whether they handle planning, the current listing status, and whether an earlier message already exists. Use a natural greeting from the actual relationship; do not invent a shared memory. Send one suitable draft, not the whole sequence. Do not bulk-message the public inventory or follow up after an opt-out.

Keep recipient lists, responses, contact details, verification evidence, and any submission IDs out of this public repository. Verify a leader's authority through an appropriate private channel before publishing contested changes. A DM alone should not automatically approve a boundary.
