## Key Provisions & Campground Rules

### 1. General Operating Hours & Access
- Early Move-In Window: **September 26 – October 6, 2026** (9:00 a.m. – 4:00 p.m.).
- Festival Season Duration: **October 8 – November 29, 2026**.
- Final Move-Out Deadline: **December 6, 2026** (by 12:00 p.m. noon).
- Vehicle movement within campground speed limit: **5 mph**.

### 2. Setbacks & Roadway Rules
- All campsite perimeters, screening, guy lines, and equipment must maintain at least **10 ft distance from all roadways**.
- Do not block roadways, fire lanes, or emergency access routes under any circumstances.

### 3. Fire Safety & Fire Pits
- Ground fires are strictly prohibited.
- Off-ground fire pits are permitted but must be no larger than **36 inches in diameter**.
- Fire pits must be kept at a safe distance from tents, RV awnings, and canvas structures.

### 4. Quiet Hours & Generators
- Quiet hours are observed across campground regions.
- Generators must adhere to noise restrictions during designated quiet hours.
- Carbon monoxide safety: Ensure generator exhaust faces away from sleeping areas, tents, RV windows/doors, and adjacent camps.

### 5. Environmental & Sanitation Rules
- No digging, trenching, or attaching hardware to trees or natural features.
- All trash must be disposed of in designated dumpsters or packed out.
- Permanent structures or landscape modifications are strictly forbidden.
