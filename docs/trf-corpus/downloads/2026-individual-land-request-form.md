## Required Submission Contents

Every individual land request submission must include:
1. Marked location on the official TRF campground map.
2. Detailed proposed plot layout with complete dimensions (width, depth, total square footage).
3. All RVs, fifth wheels, and trailers with bumper-to-bumper lengths.
4. All tow vehicles and personal vehicles with vehicle types.
5. All tents and canopy structures with exact dimensions.
6. Temporary construction, fencing, visual screening, or boundary markers.
7. Proof of required payment and qualifying 2026 Season Pass numbers.
8. Submission deadline: **September 7, 2026**.
