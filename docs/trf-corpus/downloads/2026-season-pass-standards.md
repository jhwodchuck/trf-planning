## Season Pass Camping Requirements

### 1. Land Allocation Capping
- **Deadline-driven allocation:** Season pass holders submitting before **September 7, 2026** are eligible for **up to 600 sq ft per qualifying pass**.
- Later submissions or standard allocations are capped at **525 sq ft per pass**.

### 2. Plot Aesthetics & Temporary Boundaries
- Campsite visual screening/boundaries must be temporary and removable.
- Boundary structures are required to be at least **4 ft tall** if screening is constructed.
- Residential household furniture (sofas, indoor armchairs) is prohibited in camping areas.

### 3. Equipment & Vehicle Inclusion
- All RVs, trailers, vehicles, tents, slide-outs, awnings, and generators must fit within the approved land allocation boundary unless separate offsite parking is arranged.
