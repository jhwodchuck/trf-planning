## Refunds, Disputes & Cancellations

By proceeding with a purchase from Texas Renaissance Festivals, LLC, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agree to abide by the terms outlined in this disclosure regarding refunds.

Please note that  all sales are final , and we do not offer refunds, rainchecks, or exchanges unless otherwise stated explicitly by Texas Renaissance Festivals, LLC or as required by law. This policy applies to all purchases made through our website or any authorized ticketing platforms. Texas Renaissance Festivals, LLC recommends acquiring tickets only through our authorized platforms and does not guarantee authenticity for tickets purchased, exchanged or otherwise obtained through unauthorized platforms.

Event Operations & Experience Disclaimer

This event operates rain or shine. All attractions, entertainment, vendors, and experiences are subject to change, delay, or cancellation due to weather, safety concerns, or operational conditions. No refunds or credits will be issued for such changes. We understand that unforeseen circumstances may arise that prevent you from attending an event. However, due to the nature of event planning and logistics, we are unable to accommodate refund requests, including but not limited to:

- Change in personal plans
- Travel disruptions
- Inclement weather

Disputes or Chargebacks

Disputing charges processed by or on behalf of Texas Renaissance Festivals, LLC including by its partner, Saffire or SaffireTix, may result in being blacklisted (banned) from future purchases and should not be used as a method for obtaining a “refund.”

## Contacting Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this policy or need assistance with your ticket purchase, please don't hesitate to contact our customer support team at tickets@texrenfest.com
