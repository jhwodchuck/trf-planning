"PILPO PAGGINS & THE HIDDEN RING!"
Dandruff the White has hidden Pilpo's magic ring in his Hoppit Hole, so that he can't use it. But now a threat has come to the Halfling's Shire, and Pilpo needs the ring to defend his home!

This parody of a fairly well-known book & movie franchise is an exciting, fun introduction to escape rooms, or a quick challenging diversion for seasoned 'escape artists!' 20 minutes/$19.99 per person/2 people minimum/8 people maximum, difficulty 3/5. These are all "public sessions" and you will likely be grouped with other guests.  Ages 15+.

"PILPO PAGGINS & THE DWARF MINES"

NEW FOR 2025: The Sword of Narwhal has been broken for centuries, but now with the threat of the D'orcs attacking, it is up to your 'fellowship' of adventurers to repair it in the mines of the Dwarves!

This brand-new adventure will challenge you to solve codes & puzzles, manipulate the forge, defeat security defenses, and re-forge the magic sword to help defeat evil! This is our first adventure suitable for families with kids ages 10 and up.

30 minutes/$29.99 per person/2 people minimum/6 people maximum, difficulty 3/5.
For "Private Sessions" it is a 4-ticket minimum per room.  Ages 10+.  Any group with minors must also have one paid adult parent or guardian in the room.

"AIRY BOTTER & THE ALCHEMIST'S LAB"

Professor Plumbum has lost his mind, and is going to set off an "Alchemy Bomb" using the  Philosopher's Kidney Stone  to turn the world's gold into lead! Using magic and technology, can you save the world's economy (and jewelry)?

This 'Airy Botter' adventure takes you to London in 1899 where you and your team of sorcerers must use your wits and magic to find the keys which will defuse Plumbum's bomb!

30 minutes/$29.99 per person/2 people minimum/8 people maximum, difficulty 4/5.  For "Private Sessions" it is a 4-ticket minimum per room.  Ages 15+ only.

"THE PIRATES' DILEMMA"

Your plucky band of privateers are enjoying their ill-gotten gains in your hideout when your Captain sends word the King's Guard are attempting to break in and take you all to the Tower of London! Can you hide all the rum bottles in their secret hidey-holes before the soldiers arrive and catch you red-handed?

This immersive 16th Century London room will challenge you to solve codes, discover hidden compartments, and show the King's Guard you're all just friendly folk and completely innocent of wrongdoing!
30 minutes/$29.99 per person/2 people minimum/8 people maximum, difficulty 4/5.  For "Private Sessions" it is a 4-ticket minimum per room.  Ages 15+ only.

## Escape the Hustle, Sip in Style

Step away from the revelry and into an oasis of refinement at Tea & Strumpets. Whether you're seeking a hearty High Tea or our new, Sunday-only Renaissance Tea, these unique Tea Experiences blend elegance with Renaissance magic.

From towering trees to tiny sandwiches, this is more than just a meal—it's a pause, a pleasure, and a perfectly timed potion to restore your inner nobility. Click below to explore your tea options and reserve your seats in the sky.

##  Dragon Forge Experience

Forge Your Own Metal Adventure from the Mouths of Fire Breathing Dragons
Welcome to the realm of dragons! This is not your average blacksmith demonstration; join us for an adventure in which YOU wield the hammer to forge your own piece. We bring you beyond the barrier of observer and into the forge itself. Here, you become the hero of your very own blacksmithing adventure. Your unique memento begins to glow in the breath of our fire breathing forges and is at the command of your hammer blows with the guidance of our professional smiths. An interactive, hands-on, blend of history and fantasy that is fiery forging fun for the whole family ages 6 and up. No skills needed.

Are you brave enough to stand the heat of the Dragon Forge Experience? Walk-ins welcome with availability or saveth some time and bookth online.

[About Dragon Forge Experience](https://www.dragonforgeexperience.com/)

##  Tabletop Games

Dive into a world of adventure with our thrilling tabletop games! Prove your worth in the King of the Ring Bounty Board, where you can take on action-packed bounties and compete for glory in quick 15-40 minute sessions. For a deeper narrative experience, join Tales of Esslytania: The Forgotten Election, a custom d6 system game where your choices shape the story of a turbulent historical event. If you're a fan of D&D, embark on one of our 2-hour games; The Heist of the Century or explore Balthor's Shattered Coil! Sessions include pre-generated characters tailored for different experience levels. Reserve your adventure now!

*book online or purchase seats at the Guild hall at Booth #138

##  Quests

Unsolved mystery within! A most tragic of fates is believed to have befallen our local alchemist. And of course, no one has fessed up. Due to the very public nature of this disappearance our investigators are stretched thin. Following very public lines of questioning and keeping the city publications up to date. One line of questioning however is being pushed out of the public eye. The last group of people known to have had contact with the alchemist- members of the Guild Hall itself. Take up the investigation, question the suspects- find clues and solve puzzles to ultimately discover the truth behind- The Secrets of the Fallen Alchemist.

*book online or purchase at the Guild hall at Booth #138
