#  For Lords & Ladies (21+)

##  The King's Feast

A
two hour dinner theatre event where you can feast like royalty! Come join us in
the King's Arms Feast Hall where we will delight you with a two hour variety
show filled with music, comedy, delicious food and hearty laughter! Special
guests from New Market Village will entertain as you feast your way through a
six course meal and all the libations, iced tea and water your mug can (
legally ) handle! Don't
forget to sample our famous and refreshing in house Sangria! Adorn yourself in
your finest festival wear (or whatever you're comfortable in, we don't judge!)
and join His and Her Majesty as we raise a toast to good health, fortune, and
merriment at The King's Feast!

##  Odin's Table Wine Tasting Experience

**ODIN'S TABLE RETURNS FOR THEIR FINAL YEAR!**

Haak Wines, the  Official Wine Sponsor of the Texas Renaissance Festival , invites you to experience  ODIN'S TABLE , one of TRF's most sought-after attractions, a  Texas wine tasting fit for the gods!

Curated by Haak Wines' expert staff (and perhaps an appearance from Odin himself), you'll embark on a  visually stunning, interactive, and entertaining  journey through  five distinct Haak wines . Each sip tells a story, each pour immerses you in the craftsmanship of Texas winemaking. As a final indulgence,  our signature Madeira wine will be paired with a chocolate brownie , creating the perfect finish to this legendary tasting.

Your experience includes a  commemorative wine glass  adorned with the Odin's Table and Haak Wines logos, yours to cherish long after the festival ends. While no other food is provided, you're welcome to bring your own feast to enjoy alongside the wines.

Join us at ODIN'S TABLE for a  one-of-a-kind adventure  in wine, storytelling, and revelry! Skol!

This is a 21+ event, but offspring may attend if well behaved, otherwise they will be sacrificed to the Gods!

##  Wyrmwood Public House

Welcome to Wyrmwood! TRF's first multi-sensory entertainment and
social experience, where artistic rebellion, bohemian decadence
and absinthe fantasies collide with circus, sideshow, burlesque,
magic, and more. Our (climate controlled) three-story property
offers a haven for sensory exploration, where each floor unveils a new layer
of intrigue and delight. Ascend to our craft cocktail bar, the heart of
Wyrmwood, where skilled mixologists craft exquisite libations to
tantalize your taste buds. Whether you're unveiling secrets in an absinthe
tasting, indulging in stories portrayed by our world-renowned
entertainers, or simply basking in the immersive atmosphere,
Wyrmwood promises an unforgettable experience for all your
senses.

21+ Venue - ID Required

##  The Green Hour Presents:

## The Spirits Within

Where circus defies reality, vaudeville ignites the stage, and burlesque seduces

the night.

Beneath the haze of absinthe dreams, the doors of Wyrmwood’s Liquor Cabinet

swing open, and the spirits within come out to play.

How will your favorite liquor reveal themself? Bold and fiery? Smooth and sultry?

Wild, sparkling, or delightfully dangerous? Each spirit steps from the bottle to the

stage, unveiling their true character through daring feats of spectacle and

enchantment.

Join us, for the night is young and the spirits are stirring!

21+ Venue ~ ID Required

##  The Grande Parade Matinée

## An Exclusive Balcony Gathering

Step into Le Balcon de l'Élégance, an exclusive midday
matinée at Wyrmwood Public House that immerses you in the opulent charm of
French Bohemia. As a VIP guest, ascend to our second-story balcony, a
privileged perch overlooking the vibrant daily parade. Here, the magic of the
parade unfolds beneath you, while you revel in the elevated ambiance of
Wyrmwood's decadent hideaway.

Indulge in the luxury of our decedent service, where an aerial bartender
descends gracefully to pour you the finest champagne, adding a touch of
whimsical elegance to your experience.

This is more than just a viewing—it's a celebration of the quintessential TRF
revelry, the Grande Marche Parade!

##  A Glass Affair

## An Absinthe Tasting Rendezvous

Embark on a captivating journey into the world of absinthe, where mysteries

unravel and secrets are unveiled.

This guided tasting features four distinct absinthes, prepared in the traditional

ritual with fountain and sugar, as expert hosts lead you through their flavor

profiles, origins, and infamous lore.

Immerse yourself in a sensory exploration that echoes the indulgent spirit of

historic European cabarets, all within TRF’s only climate-controlled craft

cocktail bar… an experience reserved for the most refined of the Bourgeoisie.

Times: 12:20pm, 2:20pm, & 4:20pm

*Now hosted on the first floor of booth #225!

##  The World's MOST Pub Crawl

Trade laughs and skip lines over the course of two hours as you get to know

your hosts, fellow crawlers, and the best drinking establishments that the

fairgrounds have to offer.

Can you tell the raunchiest joke? Do the most push-ups? Bust out a hidden talent

so bizarre that we never would have guessed it existed? Have a little liquid

courage and strut your stuff!

Crawler!

## Agave Tasting

Step into a world of vibrant color and savor at our immersive Agave Tasting

inside Tecuani Trading Post.
