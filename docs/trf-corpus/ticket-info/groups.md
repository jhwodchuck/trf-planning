#  Groups

Experience an exciting group outing like no other. Groups will enjoy strolling the cobblestone paths of our authentically recreated 66-acre 16th century village, visiting more than  400 shoppes  featuring unique arts and crafts as well as sampling foods from many cultures, playing games of skill and participating in a variety of human-powered rides. In addition, hundreds of costumed characters are there to entertain your party with over  200 performances daily . Each of our eight themed weekends brings a new adventure for your group, offering unique programs, activities, and more!

Groups of 15 or more adults can receive discounted admission tickets.

Email: [Tickets@TexRenFest.com](mailto:tickets@texrenfest.com) for more information.

##  Corporate Codes

- Discount on  Festival and Camping Tickets

- Minimum of  15 employees  required to qualify
To Apply  , email [Groups@TexRenFest.com](mailto:groups@texrenfest.com) with the following information:

- Company name

- Estimated employee count (for large organizations)

- Contact person

- Job title

- Company city and state

- Email Address

- Phone number

Approval Process:

- If approved, you will receive a confirmation email with your company discount code and usage instructions

- Applications must be  resubmitted annually

Important Restrictions:

- Available for purchase  online or at the festival gate  only

- Codes are intended for  employees and their families only

- For gate purchases,  ID and proof of employment  may be required

- Do  not  share codes on public social media; sharing codes publicly revokes your right to having a code.

##  Tax-Exempt Groups

For more information, email [Tickets@TexRenFest.com](mailto:tickets@texrenfest.com) and include:

- Group name (school, organization, troop number, etc.)

- Contact phone number

- Available hours for contact

- Tax exemption form

- Preferred attendance date

- Estimated number of tickets needed

- Delivery method: Pickup, Mail, Will Call, or Email

- Payment method: Card or Check
