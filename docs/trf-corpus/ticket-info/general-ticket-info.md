## Worried about bad weather or needing to switch your chosen day later on?

For an extra $5 fee, you can buy a ticket w/ exchange insurance included which allows you to pay any difference (unless it is an even exchange) and swap the ticket for another day, for any reason. This does not allow a refund of any kind. Exchanges can be done by emailing [tickets@texrenfest.com](mailto:tickets@texrenfest.com).

## Ticket FAQs

[How Much Is Admission?](javascript:void(0))

- Prices range depending on what day and whether you are buying for an adult or child.

- Tickets can be purchased at The Kingdom gates on the day of the festival or in advance online at TexRenFest.com via ETIX.com.  Tickets are not sold at any other brick-and-mortar or online outlet.

-  Discounted Advanced Tickets  are available for purchase online at TexRenFest.com.

-  Discounted Advanced Tickets  sales for a given weekend end that Friday night at midnight. Once that weekend's presale ends, tickets for that weekend will be available at the box office and online at our standard pricing for that weekend.

- Tickets purchased online are date specific and will need to be used that day only

- Kids 4 and Under are FREE everyday. No ticket necessary. Their presence will be accounted for at the gate.

- Kids 5-12 are HALF PRICE every Saturday and Thanksgiving Friday.

- Kids 5-12 are FREE every Sunday. No ticket necessary. Their presence will be accounted for at the gate.

[Are There Discounts for Groups?](javascript:void(0))

Group Discount Tickets are available to groups of 15 or more adults. Not available at the gate. For more information, email [groups@texrenfest.com](mailto:groups@texrenfest.com) or call our office at 281-356-2178.

[Do you offer a discount for senior citizens, military, law enforcement, first responders or students?](javascript:void(0))

Every Sunday, we offer discounted tickets to veterans, active-duty military, and first responders with valid ID.

We currently do not offer student or senior discounts.

[Are Tickets Date Specific?](javascript:void(0))

Tickets purchased online are date specific and will need to be used that day only

* Tickets and passes are non-refundable and non-exchangeable. The Texas Renaissance Festival operates rain or shine- no rain checks will be given

[Is TRF Doing A Sale of The Century?](javascript:void(0))

Unfortunately, Sale of the Century won't be an event that will return to TRF. We try to ensure we listen to our patrons and their feedback, but our pricing is dictated by ensuring the longevity of our festival. We've hit year 50 - and we want 50 more! In addition, we value our venues and entertainers and want to ensure they are given a space to succeed and be given the compensation we feel they rightly deserve.

[Who Do I Contact For a Missing Ticket Order?](javascript:void(0))

E-Tix Customer Support Team  at [1-800-514-3849](tel:1-800-514-3849).
E-Tix Customer Support Hours :  Monday-Saturday: 9 am to 8 pm EST  Sunday: Noon to 8 pm EST

You can look up your order and send your tickets
to your email. Please visit [www.etix.com](http://www.etix.com).
Click on the ORDER LOOKUP tab on the top right of the page.  From there
you can put in the email that you used to purchase the tickets, the last 4
digits of the card/debit card you used to purchase the ticket, and affirm you
are not a robot.  You should be able to see your ticket order and even
resend the tickets to your email address.

[Can we leave the park without paying to re-enter?](javascript:void(0))

Yes. Please have your hand stamped at the exit gate when you leave. When you re-enter the Park, show your stamped hand to the Beefeaters for re-entry. The front gates close at approximately 7 PM and you cannot re-enter after that time.

If you have any questions: email   [tickets@texrenfest.com](mailto:tickets@texrenfest.com) or call our office at 281-356-2178.
