##  Handicap Parking

Parking is accessible to current handicap sticker holders and disabled veterans. Rows range from 2-5 being the closest to the festival entrance and near pedestrian walkways for easy access. Please follow signs and the TRF parking crew will direct you where to go. Be advised you may be prompted to show

your valid credentials.

##  General Parking

Parking is available for all cars,buses, and motorcycles. The TRF parking crew will be happy to direct you to the appropriate area. There is no overnight parking after festival hours.

- Drop-Off / Pick-Up are only available in designated areas. (Including but not limited to Uber, Lyft ,Vans, etc.)

##  Paid Parking

##  Premium Parking

*NEW*   For this 2026 season, TRF is introducing  Premium Parking . It will be the closest access to parking from the entrance replacing valet parking from previous years. Now our parking crew will direct you into our designated “self-parking” areas. The system is set up to accommodate our attendees for a safe, quick

and easy faire parking experience.

A few things to keep in mind.

- Capacity is limited and will be accommodated in order of arrival.

- Parking kiosks will stop selling/receiving Premium Parking tickets at 4:00 PM or when capacity is reached.

- Vehicles are still retrievable after 4:00 PM

- Shuttle services will be available.

*DOES NOT INCLUDE FESTIVAL ADMISSION
Handicap parking is free

Tickets are non-refundable. The Texas Renaissance Festival is an outdoor event that operates rain or shine. There will be no refunds, exchanges or rain checks for purchased tickets.

##  Preferred Parking

Preferred Parking  continues to be the second closest parking to the festival entrance. It is still offering a convenient proximity to the faire.

- Parking kiosks will stop selling/receiving Preferred Parking tickets at 4:00 PM or when capacity is reached.
