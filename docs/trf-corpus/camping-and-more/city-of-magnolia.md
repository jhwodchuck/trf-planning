growth and charm. Just 12 minutes from the Texas Renaissance Festival, this

of a royal stroll.

##  History of our Official Host City

Magnolia's roots trace back to 1840 when Montgomery County's southern boundary reached Spring Creek. First called Mink in 1885, the town officially became Magnolia in 1903 and grew into a bustling lumber and farming hub with sawmills, shops, and a railroad depot. Voters incorporated Magnolia as a city on October 15, 1968, with Cedric Smith as its first mayor. The historic Depot, now at 426 Melton Street, remains a treasured centerpiece of downtown on the National Register of Historic Places.
