#  Camping Passes

##  Opening Weekend Camping Pass

**OPENING WEEKEND ONLY**

Ages 12 and under free in the campgrounds.

Advance Camping Individual Ticket: $15 per person
Weekend-Of Camping Individual Ticket: $20 per person

A camping ticket is good for one person for one festival weekend Thursday at 8 am - Monday at noon.
Includes:

- Allows access to campgrounds

- Does  NOT  include festival ticket
