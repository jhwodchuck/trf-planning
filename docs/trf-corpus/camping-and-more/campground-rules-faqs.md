##  2026 Campground Rules

GOLDEN RULE

Be considerate of others. Disorderly conduct will not be tolerated. Celebrate Responsibly.

CAMP PASS/WRISTBANDS

All individuals within the campground are required to wear a valid camping wristband for

the duration of their stay. Anyone found without a valid wristband may be subject to

purchasing an additional camping pass or removal from the Fields of New Market.

FESTIVAL TICKET

All campground patrons are required to possess and present a valid Festival Ticket or

Season Pass for at least one day of the camping weekend in order to gain entry to the Fields

of New Market.

CAMPING GATES

Entrance gates are staffed 24 hours a day during operating days.

Open from 8 a.m. Thursday to 12 p.m. Monday

CAMPING FESTIVAL ENTRANCE GATES

Walking path gates leading to the main festival entrance will only be open during operating festival days. Note that re-entry into the Festival Grounds stops at 7pm.

Open from 8 a.m. to 10 p.m. each festival day

THANKSGIVING WEEKEND HOURS

Open from 8 a.m. Wednesday to 12 p.m. Monday.

FESTIVAL PARKING

Vehicles left in Festival General Parking after 2 a.m. are subject to towing at owner expense. Festival Parking within the campground is available in Rows 1, 2 and 3 near the Festival Entrance.

QUIET HOURS

Family Camping Area:  10 p.m. - 6 a.m.

Revel Camping Area:  Midnight - 6 a.m.

No vehicle traffic after midnight.

TRASH VS ASH

Please help us in preserving Fields of New Market and letting nature flourish after our revelry!

- Metal Barrels are designated for embers and ash, no trash.

- Wooden Frames and Dumpsters are designated for trash, no ash or embers

DO NOT dump coals, embers, ash, or any fire remains on the ground or in dumpsters. Ash Barrels for dumping coals, embers, ash, and fire remains are located next to dumpsters throughout the campground.

CAMPFIRES

Ground fires are strictly prohibited. 936-727-1800 Security. Fire. EMS.

All fires must be kept enclosed in off-the-ground containers, such as above ground fire pits, BBQ pits, chimineas, or liquid fueled heating. Campfire may not be elevated by  using stones or other natural materials within campgrounds. Fire pits are restricted to a standard size of no more than 36 inches in diameter.

RESERVING AN AREA

General camping areas operate on a first-come, first-served basis. Reserving additional space for patrons who are not physically present at the Fields of New Market is prohibited. Reserved spaces are exclusively designated for Season Pass holders and those with advanced bookings. Patrons are not permitted to deny other campers access to non-reserved General Camping areas located directly adjacent to an individual campsite.

CONSTRUCTION / BOUNDARIES

All campsite improvements, including boundaries (roping off), excessive decorations, or constructions temporary or otherwise are prohibited for all General Camping patrons.

GROUNDS / TREES

No cutting, burning, decorating, or placing hardware in any natural elements. No digging. No tree-tents or hammocks. No collection or relocation of any natural or constructed TRF property.

PETS

Pets are not permitted within Fields of New Market. Service animals trained to perform specific tasks for individuals with disabilities, as defined by the Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA), are welcome. Emotional support animals, comfort animals, and therapy animals are not considered service animals under the ADA and are not allowed. Service animals must remain under control at all times. We   will   ask you to leave with no refunds being offered.

MINORS

Children 16 and under MUST be accompanied by a Parent or Legal Guardian.

BICYCLES, E-BIKES, & SCOOTERS

Bicycles are permitted in the campgrounds during daylight hours only. E-bikes and motorized scooters are not allowed; bicycles must be human-powered. Bicycle use is

prohibited at night.

PARKING & ROADWAYS

The speed limit for all patron traffic is 5 mph. Parking is permitted in the space adjacent to your campsite. Vehicles, tents, and RVs must maintain a minimum distance of 10 feet from roadways. No vehicle traffic is allowed after midnight.

DISTRIBUTION OF CONSUMABLES

For the safety of everyone involved, do not distribute and/or accept food or drinks from anyone you do not know.

STRICTLY PROHIBITED

Illegal Drugs, Alcohol Abuse, Fire Spinning/Playing, Sword/Saber Sparring, Nudity, Open Bars, Sales without Campground Vendor Permit, Fireworks, Pets, Sky Lanterns, Lazer Pointers, Drones, Airborne RC Vehicles, Motorized Recreational Vehicles (including Golf Carts, ATV's, UTV's, Dirt Bikes, etc.) are NOT Allowed.

WEAPONS

No weapons allowed. Firearms (open or concealed), explosives, fireworks, blanks, or any device that fires, launches, or detonates are Strictly Prohibited. Swords, Knives, Axes, Maces, any edged or pointed weapons MUST be Sheathed and Peace Tied at All Times, period-themed or otherwise.

936-727-1800 Security. Fire. EMS.

VIOLATIONS

Violation of any rule or policy may result in removal from the campground without a refund, at the discretion of Management or Security Personnel. Management retains final authority on all matters. The Texas Renaissance Festival and Fields of New Market is privately owned and operated.

SECURITY

The Campground is always patrolled by Uniformed Law Enforcement and Security Officers. All Local, State, and Federal Laws Apply and are Enforced.

If you are approached by someone claiming to be security that is not uniformed, please call:

EMERGENCY CALL - 936-727-1800

Security. Fire. EMS.

Contact Campground Management if you have any questions: 936-218-0972

## Campground FAQs

[Where is the festival campground located?](javascript:void(0))

The Fields of New Market Campground located adjacent to the festival site.

-  11282 County Road 302 Todd Mission, 77363.

[How can I book a rental?](javascript:void(0))

Cabins, Glamping Tents, RV Rentals, Electric and Water Hookups, Preferred RV Sites.

-  For more information and to book a rental  [https://www.texrenfest.com/p/camping-and-more/camping-reservation](https://www.texrenfest.com/p/camping-and-more/camping-reservation)

-  936-218-0972 or reservations@texrenfest.com

[What kind of camping is available? Can we bring our RV?](javascript:void(0))

The campground is dispersed camping, meaning if you bring your Tent or RV, you can place it anywhere. There are no assigned sites for General Camping. If you would like to upgrade your stay, we have water and electric hookup sites for rent as well as dry RV sites, cabins, RV rentals, and
glamping tents over at [https://www.texrenfest.com/p/camping-and-more/camping-reservation](https://www.texrenfest.com/p/camping-and-more/camping-reservation)

[What is the campground like?](javascript:void(0))

-  The Fields of New Market Campground is 230 acres of dispersed camping. Meaning there are no campsites. You can bring a your own Tent, RV/Trailer and set up your campsite anywhere 10 feet from the road ways. It is first come, first serve.

-  Camping Season Pass Holders can have roped areas and leave campsite set up for the entire season.

-  Everyone is welcome, and we have designated areas for Family Camping.

-  There will be food trucks and vendors - take a look at the campground map for locations.

-  Bonfires begin at dusk; Thursday-Sunday.

-  Porta- privy's are located throughout the campground, however we do have Restrooms and a Shower House located near campground entrance.

-  See campground rules or contact the campground office for more information.

[Are there any hotel accommodations nearby?](javascript:void(0))

There are many hotels located within a 20-30 minute driving distance to the Festival. The accommodations range from full service hotels to mid-range and economy hotels. Many offer special festival rates and some have shuttle service to and from the festival. For a complete list of preferred hotels, go to the [Lodgings](https://www.texrenfest.com/lodgings-and-transportation) page of our website.
