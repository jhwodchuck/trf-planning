# Welcome to The Fields of New Market Campground

##

##  General Information

The King and Queen welcome you to The Fields of New Market! Our 230‑acre Camping Meadow offers a one‑of‑a‑kind experience for tent campers, RV travelers, and trailer adventurers alike. Gather your family, friends, Guild, or raiding party and settle into a picturesque landscape worthy of a grand adventure!

General Camping is our most relaxed and flexible way to stay. It’s an open area with no reservations or marked sites, and you’re free to choose any spot that isn’t already part of someone else’s campsite. Everything is first‑come, first‑served, and campers may not save or block off space for others.

Should you prefer a more structured stay, reservations for upgraded accommodations can be made directly below! We Offer RV rentals of all sizes, cabins fit for royalty, and glamping experiences sprinkled with a bit of story book charm.

We truly have something for just about any traveler, explore below to begin your adventure!

Ages 12 and under are free into the campground

Campground will be open 24 hours, from Thursday at 08:00 am through Monday at 12:00 pm

Campground Office Phone Hours (During Season): Thursday - Monday: 24 Hours; Tuesday - Wednesday: 9 a.m. - 5 p.m

Campground Office Phone Hours (During Off Season): Monday - Friday, 8 a.m. - 5 p.m

IMPORTANT:  To enter the Fields of New Market Campgrounds, you will need a camping pass and a festival ticket

LOCATION:  11282 CR 302, Todd Mission, Texas 77363

CAMPING INQUIRIES & RESERVATIONS:  936-218-0972 or camping@texrenfest.com

##  There Are Many Ways You Can Camp

##  DIY Camping

The Fields of New Market Campground is 230 acres of dispersed camping. Dispersed camping means that there are no designated campspots: you can set up your tent or park your RV/trailer anywhere! The only requirement is that you must be parked and camping at least 10 feet from the roadways.

## Amenities

[Showers - "The Royal Bath House"](javascript:void(0))

The Fields of New Market Campground does have showers. The Royal Bath House is open Thursday-Sunday: 6am-12am (midnight) and Monday: 6am-11am. Shower tokens available to purchase - $5 for 8-minute shower.

[Convenience Store](javascript:void(0))

You can find things you may need at our store located at the campground's entrance or near the bonfire. They have drinks, ice, firewood and just about everything you need to make your experience enjoyable.

[Shuttles](javascript:void(0))

The Fields of New Market has a shuttle available to take you to the festival and back again, at your convenience.

Operating hours are from 8am to 10pm. Hours are subject to change.

More information coming soon.

[Dump Station](javascript:void(0))

The Fields of New Market provide a dump station at $25 per use.

Driving Directions

The Fields of New Market is located on the west side of the Texas Renaissance Festival grounds.

Look for the sign marked Festival Campground/Ballroom at the corner of FM 1774 and CR 302.

Turn west onto CR 302 and travel approximately 1 mile and the campground entrance will be on the left.
