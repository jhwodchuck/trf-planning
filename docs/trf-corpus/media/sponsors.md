## A Magical Partnership

The Texas renaissance Festival offers our partners access to a broad, but unique demographic, and highly trafficked channels to communicate with them. Whether it's through sponsorship or trade partnership, or our social media and email marketing options, we can expose your brand to a wide demographic and ensure that your company achieves strong visibility within our channels.

We also offer festival sponsors the opportunity to leverage the Texas Renaissance Festival brand on their brand channels as well. From custom marketing programs, to sweepstakes, to social media giveaways and more, we provide our Sponsors the means to execute successful promotions in-market.

Our Marketing Team looks forward to working closely with you to discuss potential opportunities and develop the perfect partnership program for your brand.

To learn more about sponsorship opportunities, please contact us by emailing us at [marketing@texrenfest.com](mailto:marketing@texrenfest.com) or by calling 281-356-2178.

##  Nexus Disposal

Since 2002, Nexus underwent a minor change in management. That conversion quickly became anything but minor. We blasted the market by broadening Houston's expectations of the traditional waste service industry. Rather than service a specific sector of Houston's market, we broadened the boundaries by servicing as far north as Conroe and Montgomery County, the east Baytown area, down south to Pearland and beyond, and to the best side that is Brookshire and East Bernard areas. Additionally, we surpassed limiting ourselves from providing only roll-off services, to expanding our ability to cater Houston's explosive growth in perpetual businesses. We now offer full service options in roll-off, front-load, and portable restroom services. We are Houston's premier waste hauler for all your municipal solid waste and construction debris needs.

[More Information](https://www.nexusdisposal.com/)

##  Haak Wines

It all began in 1969 on the 10th wedding anniversary of Raymond and Gladys Haak. Gladys's gifts to Raymond that year were two Concord grapevines. What began as a hobby with those two vines soon turned into a great passion for both novice winemakers. With the Concords and other grape varieties, Raymond tested which would survive the harsh climate conditions along the Gulf Coast of Texas.

[More Information](https://www.haakwines.com/)
