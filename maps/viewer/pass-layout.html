How this works

  • Move around: scroll wheel or trackpad pinch zooms in and out on whatever is under your pointer. Drag any empty area to pan, or hold Space (or middle-mouse) to pan even over a pass. On a touchscreen, drag to pan and pinch to zoom. The zoom slider, −/+ buttons, Fit plan and Fit aerial context all still work.
  • Drag a pass section, detached piece, or connector walkway by clicking and dragging its shape. Click empty ground to deselect.
  • Click any item in either list to select it and jump the map to it.
  • Precise placement: with a piece selected, arrow keys nudge it 0.25 ft (hold Shift for 1 ft), and [ / ] rotate it 1° (Shift for 15°). You can also type exact values into the X, Y, and rotation fields.
  • Mistakes are undoable: Ctrl+Z undoes, Ctrl+Shift+Z redoes (or use the Undo/Redo buttons).
  • Keyboard shortcuts: 0 fit plan · 9 fit aerial context · 1 100% zoom · +/− zoom · Esc deselect.
  • The badge in the lower-left corner shows the current zoom and the exact local-foot coordinate under your pointer.
  • Positions are each SVG's own top-left corner, including its small drawing margin. The committed default comes from the accepted shared arrangement. A.'s Yoga / smoking section was shifted about 2 ft and rotated 2° so it shares a boundary with the sleeping section without changing the 600 sq ft allocation.
  • Narrow planning routes: S. + S.'s 66 sq ft walkway is approximately 1.92 ft wide × 34.29 ft centerline. S. + T.'s 100 sq ft walkway is approximately 1.48 ft wide × 67.65 ft centerline. Both are allocation connectors only—not confirmed accessible paths—and must be field-checked for width, surface, slope, obstructions, and door approaches.
  • S. + T.'s movable 20 ft yurt circle is 314.16 sq ft, its portapotty end is 100 sq ft, its connector is 100 sq ft, and 85.84 sq ft remains unplaced for entry, platform-edge, anchor, rope, or setup clearance. Visible pieces plus this reserve total exactly 6,000 sq ft across camp.
  • The current arrangement autosaves to this browser's local storage as you drag. Use Your presets to save multiple named arrangements in this browser and switch between them. Use Copy share link to send an exact arrangement to someone else — no file, no repo commit, they just open the link. Use Download layout (.json) / Load from file for a portable backup, or to propose a new committed starting point (see below).
  • Each pass-section, detached-piece, and connector file under maps/overlays/passes/ is generated from python scripts/build_pass_svgs.py. Edit the source definitions and regenerate, then reload this page.
  • The muted aerial backdrop is a committed local copy of Texas NAIP 2022 (60 cm) imagery, so the layout tool no longer waits on a live GIS service. Its roughly 700 × 450 ft extent provides surrounding road, clearing, and tree context while the editable planning coordinates remain the same 230 × 200 ft area. The image is affine-warped in the browser using the same local-foot/WGS84 transform as group-site-v0.1.geojson, correcting the former ~1.68° rotation and the resulting southern-road drift. This is still planning imagery, not a survey.
  • The cyan line through 30°15'20.86"N 95°50'47.36"W and 30°15'19.63"N 95°50'47.44"W is a hard western limit. Pass sections and detached pieces are automatically kept east of its extrapolated alignment, including when rotated or loaded from an older saved layout.
