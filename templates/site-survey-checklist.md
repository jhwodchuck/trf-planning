# TRF Field Site Survey Checklist

Complete this at the requested/assigned campsite before producing the approval-quality layout.

## Bring

- [ ] 100-ft measuring tape or laser distance meter suitable for outdoor use
- [ ] 25-ft tape
- [ ] Compass/phone with calibrated compass
- [ ] Phone/GPS with location services enabled
- [ ] Stakes, cones, or removable flags permitted by TRF
- [ ] String/chalk that does not damage the site
- [ ] 4-ft level and leveling blocks
- [ ] Clipboard and printed campground map
- [ ] Camera/phone and spare battery
- [ ] Second person for long measurements

Do not dig, drive hardware, or attach anything to trees merely to perform the survey.

## Boundary and road measurements

- [ ] Mark each plot corner/reference as `P1`, `P2`, etc.
- [ ] Measure every boundary segment.
- [ ] Measure at least one diagonal; use both diagonals for a four-sided plot.
- [ ] Record bearings/directions of boundary segments where possible.
- [ ] Mark the physical road edge, not only the apparent tire track.
- [ ] Measure from road edge to nearest plot/boundary reference.
- [ ] Lay out and photograph the 10-ft roadway setback.
- [ ] Identify entrances, intersections, fire lanes, and turnaround constraints.
- [ ] Record neighboring plot boundaries or occupied objects visible at survey time.

## Elevation and drainage

Establish a temporary datum `E0 = 0.00` at one documented point.

- [ ] Record relative elevation at every plot corner.
- [ ] Record elevation along the intended camper wheel and landing-gear locations.
- [ ] Identify the high and low points.
- [ ] Mark ditches, swales, ruts, ponding, erosion, and visible flow paths.
- [ ] Look for water/debris marks indicating prior runoff.
- [ ] Record ground firmness and saturation.
- [ ] Note whether leveling blocks would redirect or obstruct drainage.
- [ ] Photograph terrain from low angle in both cardinal directions.

## Fixed obstacles and vegetation

For each tree, stump, root mass, pole, ditch, rock, or other obstacle:

- [ ] Assign an ID.
- [ ] Measure its center or outer boundary from two known references.
- [ ] Record trunk/object diameter and canopy/branch projection.
- [ ] Record lowest overhead clearance.
- [ ] Photograph it with a scale reference.
- [ ] Note protected/no-contact areas.

## Camper mock placement

Using measured deployed camper dimensions:

- [ ] Mark body corners with removable cones/flags.
- [ ] Mark nose/hitch and rear extremity.
- [ ] Mark all three slide rectangles.
- [ ] Mark entry stairs and required landing/walking area.
- [ ] Mark awning/canopy conflict zones.
- [ ] Mark landing gear, axles, and stabilizers.
- [ ] Check roadway setback using the closest deployed point, not merely the body.
- [ ] Confirm emergency-exit clearance.
- [ ] Confirm the camper can be leveled without prohibited ground alteration.

## Vehicle access test

- [ ] Record arrival direction.
- [ ] Walk or test the truck/trailer approach.
- [ ] Mark front swing and trailer tail swing.
- [ ] Verify overhead clearance.
- [ ] Identify the unhitching position.
- [ ] Verify truck escape/parking route after unhitching.
- [ ] Verify a realistic move-out path with neighboring sites occupied.
- [ ] Photograph all tight points.

## Generator feasibility test

Do not operate generators solely for this survey unless all safety requirements are already satisfied.

- [ ] Mark every camper door, operable window, and vent.
- [ ] Mark known neighboring doors/windows/vents or occupied areas.
- [ ] Draw/check 20-ft separation from openings for feasibility.
- [ ] Check manufacturer combustible clearances.
- [ ] Identify unobstructed exhaust direction.
- [ ] Identify cooling-air and service access.
- [ ] Mark proposed fuel storage separately.
- [ ] Test the proposed screen position without enclosing the generators.
- [ ] Verify cords can be routed without crossing the emergency path or roadway.
- [ ] Record wind direction only as an observation, not as a substitute for clearance.

## Canopy and fence mock placement

- [ ] Mark canopy roof corners.
- [ ] Mark legs, feet, guy lines, stakes, or ballast.
- [ ] Verify camper-door and stair clearance.
- [ ] Verify runoff from canopy roof does not discharge into doorways or generator equipment.
- [ ] Mark each fence/screen panel and foot/post.
- [ ] Mark gate opening and swing.
- [ ] Verify at least one unobstructed emergency route.
- [ ] Verify generator exhaust/cooling clearances remain open.
- [ ] Confirm no attachment to trees and no unapproved digging.

## Survey closeout

- [ ] Area calculated from measured geometry.
- [ ] Dimensions checked for transcription errors.
- [ ] Photos backed up.
- [ ] Coordinates copied in decimal degrees.
- [ ] Assumptions listed separately.
- [ ] No temporary markers or trash left onsite unless authorized.
- [ ] Data entered into the repository.
